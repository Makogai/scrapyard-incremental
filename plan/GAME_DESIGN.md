# Game Design

## Fantasy

Turn a tiny toy-like junk lot into a powerful recycling operation. A weak handheld magnet begins with cans and bolts; later upgrades pull tires, appliances, engines, doors, and whole scrap cars into increasingly impressive crushers.

Each player owns a large temporary physical scrapyard plot in the server. Saved progression rebuilds on whichever of 12 slots they receive. Other players can walk through and admire the yard, owner sign, machines, and trophies, but cannot collect, sell, purchase, or trigger owner content.

## Core Experience

The player should understand the loop by moving: nearby eligible scrap reacts, travels smoothly toward the character, fills a prominent storage bar, and directs the player toward a visible crusher when capacity is reached. The first collection occurs within 5 seconds, first sale within 20-30 seconds, and first upgrade within 30-45 seconds.

## Plot Progression

- Stage 1 Starter Yard: dirt, rusty fencing, light scrap, small storage, and a tiny back-centered crusher.
- Stage 2 Working Scrapyard: improved fencing, partial concrete, appliances/plates, conveyor, and larger storage.
- Stage 3 Vehicle Yard: industrial fencing, vehicle crusher, engines/shells, crane, containers, and multiple conveyors.
- Stage 4 Recycling Facility: clean industrial ground, automated crusher, sorting machines, robotics, warehouses, and presentation lighting.
- First prestige: targeted at 45-75 minutes. Reset regular progress for Gears and a permanent earnings multiplier.

## Upgrade Pillars

Magnet Strength gates weight tiers. Magnet Range changes pickup reach. Storage Capacity lengthens routes. Movement Speed is modest and capped. Scrap Value improves earnings. Collection Speed improves attraction feel. Each purchase shows current level, next effect, and server-calculated price.

## Collection and Variants

Each scrap type records discovery, best variant, and lifetime count. Silver, Gold, and Rainbow variants are rare visual moments with 2x, 5x, and 15x value. Variants do not bypass magnet-strength requirements.

## MVP Boundaries

Included: three areas, eight scrap types, four variants, six upgrades, selling, Gears prestige, collection book, settings, saves, responsive UI, basic effects/audio hooks.

Deferred: free-form decoration editing, pets, rebirth trees, trading, quests, codes, clans, cross-server systems, multiple worlds, elaborate vehicles, procedural terrain, and complex monetization.
