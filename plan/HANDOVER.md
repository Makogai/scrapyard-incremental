# Current world layout

- The experience supports 12 simultaneous player plots.
- Three plots line each of four short inward-facing streets around a central circular plaza.
- The center reserves visible authored spaces for leaderboards, featured purchases, daily rewards, prestige, and showcase content.
- The map is an outdoor 1000-stud island surrounded by Terrain water, with beach/cliff edging, trees, rocks, streetlights, clouds, atmosphere, and warm afternoon lighting.
- District rows sit 325 studs from the hub. This gives adjacent corner plots 15 studs of horizontal clearance; island surfaces stay at or below the plot-foundation underside to prevent clipping.
- World geometry lives directly in Studio under `Workspace/NewMap`; gameplay must not recreate it at runtime. Production plots are `Workspace/NewMap/Plots/Plot01` through `Plot06`. The retired world is recoverably archived at `ServerStorage/RetiredWorld_NewMapMigration`.
- `NewMap/AreaShop/CHS_Shop` opens `AreasScreen`, `NewMap/Magnet Shop/CHS_Shop` opens the Magnets inventory view, and `NewMap/Shop/CHS_Shop` opens the Robux shop. All are server-owned walk-in triggers with a per-player debounce.
- `PackGameplayUI/MagnetShopScreen` is a dedicated, independently editable screen with Magnet cards, card prices, and a right-side purchase panel. Advanced Magnet is bought with cash through `ShopService`, while Quantum Magnet uses its configured game pass. `InventoryScreen` remains separate and equip/unequip-only.
- `PrestigeScreen` uses exact authored bindings for current/next rebirth, current/projected Gear money multipliers, cash requirement progress, and the server-authoritative rebirth action. The normal button is disabled until eligible and displays its calculated Gear reward; Skip Rebirth is a separately priced repeatable purchase.
- Skip Rebirth is now the repeatable `SkipRebirth` developer product (fallback R$149). A confirmed receipt completes one rebirth, grants its minimum normal Gear reward, resets progression, refreshes the plot/scrap, and is receipt-idempotent. Maintain the full catalog, Marketplace IDs, image status, and icon prompts in `plan/ROBUX_STORE.md`.
- `PackGameplayUI` uses full-screen coordinates (`IgnoreGuiInset=true`, `ScreenInsets=None`). Do not add the CoreGui inset to direct children: Roblox already accounts for it visually, and applying it again pushes the top controls down while clipping the bottom HUD.
- `NewMap/RichestPlayer` displays the richest currently connected player by cash. `RichestPlayerService` polls authoritative public state every two seconds and swaps a non-colliding avatar statue only when the winner changes. The statue now stands on the `CharacterPedestal` model rather than the old `Podium` part, and the presentation lives on that model's four tablets: each `Tablet<n>` child gets a `RichestPlaque` SurfaceGui built at runtime on its geometrically-derived outward face, reading RICHEST PLAYER / name / cash. Nothing is matched by path beyond those names, so the pedestal can be moved, rotated or rebuilt. Flushness is enforced continuously -- the soles are aligned to the disc top every frame, which holds for any avatar, scale, rig or accessory and replaced a hand-tuned 3.45-stud `HeightOffset`. `HeightOffset` is `-1.05`, the median of the dance's foot travel, so the statue reads as planted rather than touching down for one frame per cycle. Plaques sit on thin aligned `PlaqueSurface` helper parts rather than on the tablets directly, and nothing rotates a GuiObject. Six Roblox behaviours had to be worked through: unanchored pedestal parts scattering under physics at Play start; `Model:ScaleTo` not re-posing limbs in the same frame; the dance pose sitting lower than the idle pose it was measured against; the tablets' largest face being their top, not the disc rim; a rotated GuiObject rendering unpredictably; and a part at `Transparency = 1` not drawing its SurfaceGui at all. Tunable attributes: `DanceAnimationId` (required), `AvatarScale`, `HeightOffset` (now 0), `RotationSeconds`. Roblox's built-in nameplate is off since the plaques and the `OverheadNameplate` already carry the text. `EditorPreview` is still a baked preview frame removed during Play. Full setup notes in `docs/STUDIO_SETUP.md`.
- Scrap size and placement are data-driven. `ScrapConfig.Size` was declared for every scrap and never read; it is now the footprint an authored model is normalised to (uniform scale, longest axis to longest axis), with an optional `ModelScale` multiplier per scrap on top. The authored can arrived 3.08 studs tall against a declared 1.7 and now lands at 1.7. Generic models in `ServerStorage.ScrapModels` were built to match `Size` already and are left alone. Placement measures the model's bounding box after scaling and rests its underside on the spawn marker's top surface plus `SCRAP_HOVER` (0.06), replacing a flat 1.5-stud lift from the marker centre that floated small models and buried big ones -- verified at +0.0600 on every live piece, with collection still working. The code deliberately does not rotate models: several are modelled edge-on and stand up like walls, which is a Studio fix.
- Plot owner signs are AUTHORED, not rendered. The card lives in the place file at `OwnerSign.Board.PlotSignPlate.PlotSignSurface.Card`; `PlotSignController` only writes text and the headshot into named children, so restyling is a Studio job. The React `PlotSign` screen and its story were deleted -- a rendered card could not be seen or positioned in Edit and needed a stand-in preview, which always drifts. The server publishes `Occupied/OwnerUserId/OwnerName/Rebirths/Money/TotalScrap` as attributes on the sign and `PlotService` refreshes them every 3s. The plate must sit ~0.2 studs off the board's face: measured, a SurfaceGui directly on the board did not draw in game at all, nor did one 0.06 off, while all three rendered fine in Edit -- so Edit is not a reliable check for world SurfaceGuis. The post was moved 0.3 studs back so it no longer cuts the card. One client-side `YOUR PLOT` marker floats above the owner's own plot floor. Full notes in `docs/STUDIO_SETUP.md`.
- The COLLECTION index renders scrap as real 3D models. `ScrapVariants.PublishPreviews` copies them to `ReplicatedStorage.ScrapPreviews` as `<ScrapId>_<VariantId>` plus a plain `<ScrapId>` generic fallback, stripping ParticleEmitters and Highlights because a ViewportFrame draws neither. `ModelView` gained a `scrap` repository and accepts a candidate list; `GameData.scrapIndex` asks for the best rarity found, then Normal, then the generic name. Undiscovered entries render the real model blacked out. Nebula previews read dark because those models rely on emitters that a ViewportFrame will not draw; the cell's rarity stripe carries the tier. Stories show glyphs instead of models, because publishing needs a server.
- Scrap art is authored per rarity in `Workspace.Scraps/<Type>/<Rarity><Name>` and resolved by `ScrapVariants`. The model's own part colours and ParticleEmitters ARE the rarity treatment: `ScrapService` clones it and skips its procedural tint and emitter entirely. Rarity comes from the model name's prefix; the folder is matched to a scrap id by `ModelName`/`Id` with suffix tolerance (pairing `Can` with `MetalCan`), overridable by a `ScrapId` attribute, and refused rather than guessed when ambiguous. Types or rarities with no art fall back to the generic model in `ServerStorage.ScrapModels` plus the procedural look, so the set fills in one model at a time -- `Tire` has only Normal today. At startup the service moves the folder to `ServerStorage` and anchors every part, and logs what it indexed plus anything it could not place. Setup notes in `docs/STUDIO_SETUP.md`.
- `NewMap/Decorations/StairsLeaderboard/Leaderboards` contains authored Top Playtime, Top Rebirths, and Top Robux Spent boards. `LeaderboardService` updates ten persistent ordered-data-store entries every 60 seconds; Studio uses isolated development rankings. Confirmed developer-product receipts and completed game-pass purchases feed the Robux board without double-counting replayed receipts.
- `PackGameplayUI/Background/TeleportButtons` requests server-validated teleports to authored `NewMap/TeleportDestinations` markers for Leaderboards and Shop, or dynamically to the player's assigned plot spawn.
